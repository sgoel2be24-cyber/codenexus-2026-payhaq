01 / The problem

Visibility without action.

India gave micro and small suppliers a legal remedy for late payments. Most never use it.

45 days
The legal payment window for micro and small suppliers under Section 15 of the MSMED Act, 2006.
3×
The RBI bank rate, compounded monthly, that a delayed buyer owes under Section 16.

A supplier can prove the invoice, the delivery and the delay. What they lack is the time to reconcile payments, the evidence discipline to build a claim, and the legal support to file it. So the aging report stays a report.

What it costs today
  • Working capital locked in receivables; payroll and supplier payments wait.
  • Statutory interest never claimed — the buyer gets an interest-free loan.
  • Invoices discounted at 2–4% a month to get cash sooner.
  • The relationship still strains, because the chase is manual and emotional.

The problem statement

Indian micro and small enterprises can legally demand payment within 45 days and compound interest for delays, but most lack the time, evidence discipline, and legal support to convert an overdue invoice into a recovery action.

Who holds the problem
  • Micro and small B2B suppliers — 5–50 employees, ₹50L–₹5Cr turnover.
  • Owner-operators selling to larger buyers on 60–120 day cycles.
  • CA / AR managers handling 20–100 MSME clients.
  • Cluster associations whose members face the same buyers and the same delays.
Early wedge: precision manufacturing clusters in Ludhiana, Pune, Coimbatore and Rajkot — dense, document-heavy, and used to 60–120 day terms.
Scope discipline
What we take on
  • B2B invoices from micro and small suppliers
  • 45+ day delays and evidence gaps
  • Statutory interest, demand notice and Samadhaan claim
  • Follow-up, escalation and recovery tracking
What we do not
  • Consumer debt or legal representation
  • Arbitration strategy or recovery from insolvent buyers
  • Lending, credit or invoice financing
Sources: MSMED Act, 2006, ss. 15, 16 and 18; MSME Samadhaan portal; RBI bank rate. Interest figures in this document are illustrative, not a promise of recovery.

03 / The solution

An agent that executes recovery.

PayHaq is the action layer between an aging report and a recovered invoice.

One line

Upload your ledger. PayHaq reconciles payments, finds invoices past 45 days, computes statutory interest, audits the evidence, drafts the notice and Samadhaan claim, files it, and follows up — with you approving every send.

Why an agent is required
01
Multi-step, multi-week

Reconcile → audit → draft → file → track. A single prompt cannot own a workflow that spans weeks.

02
Real tool use

Ledger parser, OCR, interest calculator, legal retrieval, document generator and portal automation.

03
Memory that matters

Buyer history, past promises, correspondence and evidence status — remembered across cases.

04
Human-in-the-loop

Irreversible actions need approval. Every step is logged, reviewable and reversible before send.

The original angle

Every claim carries an evidence graph. Each amount and each legal assertion is traceable to a document, a section of the MSMED Act, and a timestamp. The agent cannot send a notice it cannot prove.

04 / Product workflow

From ledger to recovery.

Five stages, five agents, three human gates.

01

INGEST & RECONCILE

Meet the owner where the data already is.

  • CSV / XLSX or Tally export
  • Match invoices to payments
  • Handle partials and credit notes
  • Flag ambiguous matches
Agent · Reconciler
02

DETECT & AUDIT

Turn an aging bucket into a legal case.

  • 45+ days, micro/small only
  • PO, invoice, challan, e-way bill
  • Bank credit and correspondence
  • Evidence completeness score
Agent · Evidence Auditor
03

QUANTIFY & DRAFT

Make the claim specific and defensible.

  • Principal + statutory interest
  • 3× RBI bank rate, monthly
  • Cited demand notice
  • Samadhaan claim statement
Agent · Legal Drafter
04

APPROVE & FILE

The owner stays in control.

  • Review every notice
  • Choose tone: preserve or firm
  • Send email / registered post
  • Prepare Samadhaan packet
Human gate · Approve
05

TRACK & RECOVER

Recovery is a follow-through game.

  • Follow-up cadence
  • Classify buyer responses
  • Escalate to Facilitation Council
  • Track settlement and cash
Agent · Tracker

06 / Technical plan

Reliable by design.

The hard part is not the chat. It is proving the claim.

Stack · and why
LayerChoiceWhy
FrontendNext.js · TypeScript · Tailwind · shadcn/uiFast dashboard, typed contracts, clean document viewer
BackendFastAPI · Python 3.12Ledger/document libraries, typed schemas, async jobs
AgentLangGraph · OpenAI GPT-4.1/4oStateful, resumable workflow with human-in-the-loop nodes
DataPostgres + pgvector · Redis · S3One datastore for cases, evidence and legal retrieval
DocumentsReact-PDF / WeasyPrintVersioned notice and claim templates
ObservabilityLangfuse · SentryTrace every agent step; catch failures before the demo
Reliability by design
Deterministic interest engine

Pure Python + Decimal. 3× RBI bank rate, monthly compounding. Unit tests against worked examples. No LLM arithmetic.

Legal RAG with citations

MSMED Act, Samadhaan guidelines, RBI notifications. Section-level metadata. “No citation, no claim.” Last-verified date on every rule.

Evidence graph

Invoice → PO → challan → e-way bill → bank credit. A missing link becomes an evidence gap, not a guess.

Hardest problem

Messy ledgers and legal accuracy. Deterministic matching + confidence scores; the user resolves ambiguous matches; human approval at every irreversible step.

Evaluation harness

50 synthetic ledgers with known ground truth. Metrics: reconciliation accuracy, overdue recall, interest exactness, citation correctness, notice completeness, time-to-notice. Security: encryption at rest and in transit, role-based access, data minimisation, no training on user data.

Latency budget
StepTarget
Ledger parse< 30 seconds
Reconcile + exceptions< 60 seconds
Evidence audit< 2 minutes
Draft notice + claim< 30 seconds
Human reviewAs long as the owner needs
Samadhaan filing · Round 3< 5 minutes, with confirmation
Guardrails
  • No citation, no claim. Every legal assertion points to a source.
  • No approval, no send. The agent cannot email, file or settle alone.
  • No LLM math. Interest is computed by a deterministic engine.
  • No training on user data. Documents stay inside the tenant.
  • Every action is audited. Who saw what, when, and what changed.
