**Project:** PayHaq — the agent that gets MSMEs paid.

PayHaq is an agentic recovery desk for Indian micro and small suppliers. It turns overdue B2B invoices into evidence-backed demand notices and MSME Samadhaan claims: reconcile the ledger, detect invoices past the 45-day limit, compute statutory compound interest at 3× the RBI bank rate, audit the evidence, draft a cited notice and claim, file it, and track recovery. The owner approves every irreversible step. Round 1 covers the problem, users, solution, workflow, features, technical plan, three-round development plan, impact and team.
